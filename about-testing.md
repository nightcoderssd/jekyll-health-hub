# What to expect at a testing visit

1. **Check in.** You can use a first name only.
2. **Talk with a staff member.** They explain the test and answer questions.
3. **Get tested.** Most tests take a few minutes.
4. **Get your result.** Staff tell you what it means and what to do next.

Your result is private. Staff will not judge you or share your result without your permission.
